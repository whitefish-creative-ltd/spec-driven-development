### Context

### Options considered

| Option | Pros | Cons |

Include the option you rejected. A log that only records the winner is marketing.

### Decision

What we chose. Stated plainly, in the active voice, with the person or group who chose it.

### Consequences

What this makes easy. What this makes hard. What it forecloses. What we'll have to live with,
including the parts we don't like, a decision with no downside listed hasn't been examined.

### Revisit when

The condition that would make this wrong. Load, scale, cost, a dependency changing, a
constraint softening. Optional, but a decision with a trigger is worth twice one without.
